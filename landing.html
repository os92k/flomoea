What the bot actually does

Built to manage risk first

No signals to interpret, no charts to watch — just rules the bot enforces on your account, every trade, without exception.

Session-aware execution

The bot only trades during defined high-quality market windows and sits out thin, choppy hours — timing is part of the strategy, not an afterthought.

News-event protection

High-impact releases automatically pause new entries before and after the event, so the bot is never caught trading through a spike.

Multi-layer risk controls

Hard stop-loss ceiling, breakeven, trailing stop, and a daily profit/loss limit that flattens every position the moment it's hit.

We built this the way we'd want a bot running on our own account to work.

Trade-only access

The EA runs entirely inside your own MT5 terminal, using your own broker login. We never see, request, or store your broker password at any point — our license system only ever checks your MT5 account number.

Encrypted, not exposed

We never handle your broker password at all. Your license file only stores your MT5 account number, and it's obfuscated on our end so it isn't readable at a glance.

Isolated per account

Each license is locked to a single MT5 account number. Nothing about your setup is shared with or visible to any other user.
